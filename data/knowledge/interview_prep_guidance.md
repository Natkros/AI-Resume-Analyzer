# Interview Preparation Guidance

## Technical questions
Should be scoped to skills the candidate's resume already claims (matched
skills), not to every technology mentioned in the JD — asking about a
missing skill as if the candidate has it produces an unfair, ungrounded
interview question.

## Project questions
The strongest project questions probe architecture decisions, trade-offs,
and evaluation methodology ("why did you choose X over Y", "how did you
measure success") rather than asking the candidate to restate what the
resume bullet already says.

## Behavioral questions
Should be calibrated to the seniority implied by the JD: entry-level roles
warrant questions about learning and collaboration; senior/staff roles
warrant questions about technical leadership, ambiguity, and cross-team
influence.

## Common RAG system interview topics
Chunking strategy and chunk size trade-offs, embedding model choice,
retrieval evaluation (recall@k, MRR, nDCG), handling retrieval failures
(no relevant chunk found), hallucination mitigation, and grounding /
citation of sources in the generated answer.
